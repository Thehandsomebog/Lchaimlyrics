Birthday Song FAQs

Common questions about custom birthday songs.

Can I order a birthday song for a milestone birthday?

Yes. Milestone birthdays like 30th, 40th, 50th, 60th, and 70th are some of the best occasions for a custom song because there is so much life story, family history, and personality to draw from.

Can multiple people contribute details for the birthday song?

Absolutely. Many birthday songs are group gifts, so friends, siblings, spouses, children, or coworkers can all contribute stories and memories to make the song even more personal.

What music styles work for birthday songs?

We can create birthday songs in any style—pop, rock, hip-hop, country, acoustic, or whatever matches their taste. Just let us know their preferences!
