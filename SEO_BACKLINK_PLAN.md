## 30-day execution plan

### Week 1: prepare proof

- Obtain permission to publish one anonymized or named customer story and a short audio excerpt.
- Create a one-paragraph company description, a square logo, two approved audio examples, and a consistent contact block.
- Record the exact URL, contact, status, follow-up date, and result for every prospect.

### Week 2: directories

- Apply to the two most relevant free or editorially reviewed Jewish wedding directories.
- Avoid low-quality general directories, sitewide paid links, and listings with no real audience.

### Week 3: editorial pitches

- Pitch Jewish Wedding Info with a unique article such as “How to Turn a Couple's Story Into a Jewish Wedding Song Without Losing the Tradition.”
- Identify five planners, photographers, videographers, or DJs whose work clearly includes Jewish celebrations and offer one genuinely useful expert contribution.

### Week 4: real-event feature

- With permission, assemble a customer case study containing the story behind the song, where it appeared in the event, approved photography, and one audio clip.
- Submit only one publication at a time when exclusivity is required.

## Quality and safety rules

- Do not add L'Chaim Lyrics links to Wikipedia. Wikipedia citations must support encyclopedia content with independent, reliable sources; a self-serving link is likely to be removed and can damage trust.
- Do not exchange money for followed editorial links or use private blog networks.
- Favor branded or natural anchors such as “L'Chaim Lyrics,” the article title, or the plain URL. Avoid repeating exact-match commercial anchor text.
- Confirm permission for every customer name, photo, story detail, and audio excerpt.
- A smaller number of relevant, reviewed links is more valuable than a large batch of unrelated directory links.

## Success measures

- First 30 days: 10 personalized contacts, 2 qualified directory applications, 1 editorial pitch, 1 customer-story asset in preparation.
- First 90 days: 3–5 live links from relevant sites, at least one editorial link, and measurable referral visits.
- Review monthly in Search Console: new linking sites, destination pages, branded-query growth, and ranking changes for positions 8–20.
