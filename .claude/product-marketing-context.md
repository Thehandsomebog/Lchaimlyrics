# Product Marketing Context

*Last updated: February 2, 2026*

## Product Overview
**One-liner:** Custom, studio-quality songs for Jewish celebrations

**What it does:** L'Chaim Lyrics creates personalized songs that tell customers' unique stories. Customers share names, memories, and inside jokes; we deliver a professionally produced song in their chosen style within 5-7 days.

**Product category:** Custom song creation / Personalized music service

**Product type:** Service (creative/digital product)

**Business model:** One-time purchase, 3 tiers
- Essential: $99 (1 revision, MP3, 5-7 days)
- Simcha Special: $189 (3 revisions, MP3+WAV, 5-7 days) — Most Popular
- L'Dor V'Dor: $299 (5 revisions, MP3+WAV, 72-hour priority)

**Current stage:** Pre-launch, seeking first customers

## Target Audience

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Generic music doesn't capture personal stories or carry emotional meaning for life's biggest moments.

**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Hiring a songwriter is expensive ($500-2000+) and slow
- DIY options sound amateur
- Generic "personalized" songs just swap in names without real storytelling
- Stock music has no personal connection

**What it costs them:** A missed opportunity to create a lasting memory; settling for forgettable moments at once-in-a-lifetime celebrations.

**Emotional tension:** "I want this celebration to be special and memorable, but I don't know how to make it truly unique."

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct competitors:** Other custom song services (Songfinch, etc.)
- Fall short: Not focused on Jewish celebrations, no Kol Isha options, less cultural understanding

**Secondary competitors:** Hiring a local musician/songwriter
- Falls short: Expensive, slow, unpredictable quality

**Indirect competitors:** Traditional gifts (jewelry, trips, keepsakes)
- Falls short: Don't capture the personal story; can't be experienced at the celebration

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- Jewish celebration expertise (understands the culture, terminology, religious considerations)
- Kol Isha option (male/female vocalist choice for Orthodox observance)
- Multiple music styles (pop, oriental, acoustic, techno, house, ballad, folk)
- Privacy guaranteed (songs never shared, resold, or used elsewhere)
- Fast turnaround (5-7 days standard, 72-hour rush available)

**Why customers choose us:**
- Cultural fit — we understand Jewish celebrations
- Speed — ready before their event
- Privacy — their personal stories stay private
- Quality — studio-quality, not AI-sounding

|-----------|----------|
| "Will it sound cheesy?" | We're storytellers first. Share real memories and inside jokes — the song reflects what you give us. Listen to our samples to hear the quality. |

- Non-celebration use cases

## Switching Dynamics
**Push (away from current state):**
- Generic music feels impersonal at meaningful celebrations
- Traditional gifts don't create shared experiences
- DIY attempts sound amateur

**Pull (toward us):**
- A truly unique, one-of-a-kind gift
- Something the whole celebration can experience together
- A keepsake they can replay for years

**Habit (keeps them stuck):**
- "We've always just used regular music"
- "I've never heard of custom songs for events"

**Anxiety (about switching):**
- "What if it doesn't sound good?"
- "What if they don't capture our story right?"
- Addressed by: samples, revisions included, testimonials
