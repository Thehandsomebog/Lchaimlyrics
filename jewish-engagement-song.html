Engagement Song FAQs

Common questions about custom engagement songs.

Should the engagement song be a surprise or something we create together?

Both approaches work. Some couples create the song together, while others use it as a surprise from one partner or from the family at the vort, L'Chaim, or engagement party.

What details make an engagement song work well?

The strongest engagement songs include how you met, first impressions, the proposal story, inside jokes, family connections, and why this relationship felt different from the start.
