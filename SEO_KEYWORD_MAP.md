# L'Chaim Lyrics SEO Keyword Map

Updated: 2026-07-22

## How to use this map

Each indexable page owns one primary search intent. When refreshing a page, keep its title, H1, introduction, internal-link anchors, and calls to action focused on that intent. Closely related phrases are supporting terms; they do not need separate pages unless the searcher's goal is meaningfully different.

Search Console data below is directional because the site is still early and impression counts are small. Search volume and keyword difficulty require a third-party dataset such as Ahrefs or Semrush before being labeled as high-volume or low-difficulty.

## Measurement cadence

- Monthly: export Search Console queries and pages; flag pages with at least 50 impressions and CTR below the site's average.
- Monthly: flag queries ranking positions 8–20 and improve the existing assigned page before creating anything new.
- Quarterly: review page overlap, internal links, titles, descriptions, and `dateModified` on genuinely refreshed articles.
- After 90 days of meaningful data: validate the backlog with current volume, difficulty, and business-value estimates before publishing at scale.
