What Happens Next

Once your brief and payment are complete, we can prepare your song. If you are unsure either step went through, contact us before paying again.

1. We review your brief

We read through the occasion, names, memories, style, timeline, and vocalist preference you submitted.

2. We write and produce

We turn your story into a studio-quality track with the style and vocalist choice that fits your celebration.

3. You receive your song

Your custom song is delivered privately, with revisions included based on your package.

Want to Add Anything?

Specific memories and details always land better than generic summaries. If something comes to mind, email it to us.
