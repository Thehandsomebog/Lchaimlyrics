# L'Chaim Lyrics SEO audit — July 22, 2026

## Executive summary

The site has early search visibility but a click-through problem: Google Search
Console reports 666 impressions, 2 clicks, a 0.3% CTR, and an average position
of 17.2 for the last three months. The strongest immediate opportunities are:

1. Publish the L'Chaim party guide for the site's largest underserved query.
2. Shorten title tags on high-impression service pages so their value is visible
   before Google truncates the result.
3. Publish the Bar/Bat Mitzvah montage guide and connect it to the existing
   service and planning pages.
4. Refresh older articles with substantive contextual links and accurate
   `dateModified` values.
5. Keep pages with impressions. Do not treat zero clicks alone as a reason to
   delete a page.

## Deadweight assessment

No indexable page currently qualifies for deletion. The pages with no clicks
are receiving impressions and are therefore candidates for better intent
matching, titles, and content—not deletion. The questionnaire and thank-you
pages are already excluded from normal indexing, and verification files are not
listed in the sitemap.

Before deleting any future page, check impressions, backlinks, conversions,
canonical status, and whether a stronger page can absorb its intent with a 301
redirect.

## Wikipedia dead-link research

The current Jewish wedding, Klezmer, Secular Jewish music, Erev Shel Shoshanim,
Hava Nagila, and Hora articles do not contain visible dead-link markers.

Broader Wikipedia source searches found two nearby candidates, but neither can
legitimately be replaced with a L'Chaim Lyrics page:

| Wikipedia article | Dead source | Assessment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Badchen | A dead Florida Atlantic University Recorded Sound Archives album link | A commercial custom-song page cannot replace a historical sound-archive source. Use an archive or equivalent institutional catalog. |
| Henna | A dead FDA citation concerning black-henna safety | The L'Chaim Lyrics planning guide is not a medical or regulatory source. Use the live FDA source or an archive. |

Wikipedia explicitly advises site owners to avoid adding links to sites they
own or represent for SEO. If L'Chaim Lyrics later publishes an independently
reviewed, uniquely valuable resource that directly supports a claim, disclose
the relationship and suggest it on the article's talk page for an independent
editor to assess.

1. CTR and average position for the seven rewritten service-page titles.
2. Impressions transferred from the engagement page/blog index to the new
   L'Chaim party guide.
3. Query growth for montage, candle-lighting, henna music, and anniversary
   terms.
4. Pages with impressions but zero clicks after excluding irrelevant queries.
5. New crawl, indexation, canonical, or structured-data warnings.
